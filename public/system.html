Your Complete Disney Cruise Planning Guide

Cierra Ray
Travel Expert

Planning your first Disney Cruise can be overwhelming, but with the right preparation, it becomes an exciting adventure. Here's everything you need to know.

When to Book Your Cruise

The best time to book your Disney Cruise is 6-9 months in advance. This gives you the best selection of cabins and sailing dates while also allowing you to take advantage of early booking discounts.

Choosing Your Ship

Disney currently has five ships in their fleet, each offering unique features and itineraries. Consider your destination preferences and family needs when selecting.
