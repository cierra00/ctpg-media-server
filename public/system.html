FAQ / Accordion Block

The best time to book a cruise is typically 6-9 months in advance. This gives you the best selection of cabins and sailing dates while still allowing you to take advantage of early booking discounts. However, last-minute deals can also offer significant savings if you're flexible with dates and cabin selection.

Most cruise fares include your stateroom, main dining room meals, buffet meals, select beverages, entertainment, and most onboard activities. Extras typically include specialty dining, alcoholic beverages, spa services, shore excursions, and gratuities.

For closed-loop cruises (starting and ending at the same U.S. port), U.S. citizens can travel with just a birth certificate and government-issued photo ID. However, we strongly recommend having a passport for all cruises as it provides more flexibility and is required for fly-cruise vacations.

Frequently Asked Questions

The best time to book a cruise is typically 6-12 months in advance for the best cabin selection and pricing. However, last-minute deals can sometimes offer great savings if you're flexible with dates and cabin choices.

It depends on your itinerary. For closed-loop cruises (round-trip from US ports), a passport card or birth certificate may suffice. However, we always recommend bringing a passport for international travel emergencies.

Most cruise fares include accommodations, main dining room meals, buffet access, entertainment, and basic activities. Specialty dining, alcoholic beverages, shore excursions, and spa services typically cost extra.

Most cruise lines charge automatic gratuities of $12-16 per person per day. This covers your room steward, dining staff, and other crew members. Additional tips for specialty services are at your discretion.
