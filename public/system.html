We recommend booking 6–12 months in advance for the best cabin selection and pricing, especially for popular Caribbean and Bahamas sailings.

A closed-loop cruise technically only requires a birth certificate and government ID, but we strongly recommend a full passport for all passengers for flexibility and safety.

The best time to book a cruise is typically 6-9 months in advance. This gives you the best selection of cabins and sailing dates while still allowing you to take advantage of early booking discounts. Frequently Asked Questions

The best time to book a cruise is typically 6-12 months in advance for the best cabin selection and pricing. However, last-minute deals can sometimes offer great savings if you're flexible with dates and cabin choices.

It depends on your itinerary. For closed-loop cruises (round-trip from US ports), a passport card or birth certificate may suffice. However, we always recommend bringing a passport for international travel emergencies.

Most cruise fares include accommodations, main dining room meals, buffet access, entertainment, and basic activities. Specialty dining, alcoholic beverages, shore excursions, and spa services typically cost extra.

Most cruise lines charge automatic gratuities of $12-16 per person per day. This covers your room steward, dining staff, and other crew members. Additional tips for specialty services are at your discretion.
